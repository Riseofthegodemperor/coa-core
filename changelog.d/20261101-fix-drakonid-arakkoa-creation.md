title: Drakonid and Arakkoa characters can now be created
---
Horde Drakonid (race 98) and Arakkoa (race 79) characters could not be created due to missing spawn location data. Drakonid (races 98 and 100) and 24 other custom races could not be created with Ascension custom classes because 117 racial spell definitions were absent from the world database. All issues are now fixed.
